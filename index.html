Do I need any coding experience?

No. The Java foundations course starts with programming basics. You’ll need a laptop or desktop, an internet connection, and time to practise.

Are the classes online or offline?

Classes are live and online. You can join from any city. Contact us for the meeting platform and joining instructions.

What are the class timings?

Classes are completely online and take place three times a week. You receive an assignment after every class and personal TA support at the weekend. Call us for batch timings, teaching language, course length, and the full schedule.

Do I have to take every course?

No. Choose an individual course, or take the complete seven-course package for ₹27,999. The individual courses total ₹33,993, so the package saves ₹5,994. Call us to discuss the learning sequence and schedule.

What do the mentor credits include?

Each course includes three complimentary mentor-session credits for additional guidance. Weekend TA support is separate. The seven-course package includes 21 credits in total. Call us to confirm session length, booking arrangements, and credit validity.
